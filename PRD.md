## MVP boundary

After selection, specify one primary user, one core journey, and the minimal Arc operation needed to deliver the result. List explicit exclusions and keep the demo narrow. Add AI, custom contracts, authentication, indexing, bridging, or a database only when the selected result requires them.

## Acceptance gates

- The status-quo problem and beneficiary are concrete.
- The smallest feasibility experiment passes with recorded evidence.
- Each kept feature has a criterion mapping and exact expected output.
- A fresh checkout can reproduce the documented setup and demo.
- Testnet rehearsal succeeds before any mainnet rollout.
- The live deployment runs on Arc mainnet; failures and limitations are accurately documented.
- The public-source requirement, funding, and participant declarations are resolved before submission.

Nothing in this brief establishes product functionality, user traction, project selection, or submission readiness. Track those separately in [STATUS](docs/STATUS.md) and [EVIDENCE](docs/EVIDENCE.md).
