# Arc Microgrants requirements

Last checked: **8 October 2026**. Primary source: [official DoraHacks program page](https://dorahacks.io/hackathon/arc-microgrants/detail), read directly in the browser. This is a factual project checklist; the live organizer page controls if requirements change.

## Submission requirements

- A project deployed and working on Arc **mainnet**, with a live link reviewers can open.
- A **public** source repository.
- A short explanation of the project and Arc's role in it.
- A public builder profile: GitHub, X, or Farcaster.

Design-only mockups, slide decks, testnet-only builds, projects without an Arc component, and work already funded through Circle or Arc are excluded. Prototypes, technical experiments, small apps, and continued hackathon work can qualify. The page does not require traction, incorporation, a pitch deck, or a roadmap.

## Published selection criteria

| Criterion | Evidence to prepare after selecting an idea |
| --- | --- |
| Relevance to Arc | Explain the necessary Arc interaction and demonstrate it on mainnet |
| Technical credibility | Reproducible setup, meaningful tests, real transaction/read evidence, clear limitations |
| Quality of the build | A complete narrow user journey, accurate outputs, visible failure handling |
| Worth taking further | A concrete beneficiary, useful result, and credible next experiment |

The page says shortlisted projects are scored, but publishes no score weights or detailed rubric. This evidence mapping is our planning interpretation, not an organizer scoring formula.

## Eligibility and ownership

The program accepts individuals and teams worldwide, subject to sanctions and restricted-jurisdiction screening. Pseudonymous submission is possible; selected recipients undergo private verification for payout. Only one submission per project is allowed, although teams may submit distinct projects. Entrants must own the work or hold submission rights. Ownership stays with the builder; the page states no equity, IP assignment, or exclusivity is required.

These are program-level facts. Participant-specific eligibility and any submission declarations remain unverified until the owner reviews them.

## Costs and unresolved policies

Arc uses USDC for gas; real mainnet deployment and transactions need funding. This scaffold requires no wallet and performs no paid operation. No program entry fee is stated on the detail page; this does not establish zero build or service costs.

The detail page does not specify an AI-assistance policy, minimum age, maximum team size, compulsory video length, approved open-source license, or treatment of private repositories with reviewer access. Do not infer these details. Recheck the actual submission form and organizer answers before making declarations. A video can support evidence but is not listed as mandatory here.
